YouTube Shopping affiliate program overview & eligibility - Computer - YouTube Help Skip to main content YouTube Shopping affiliate program overview & eligibility YouTube Shopping Affiliate Program The YouTube Shopping affiliate program gives you a way to earn money on YouTube while helping your audience find products they love. Through this program, you can: Help your fans discover: When you tag products in your content, fans can quickly scan for helpful information and keep watching your video while they checkout on the retailer’s site. Get more from your YouTube business: The affiliate program helps get your great shopping content in front of more brands and viewers across YouTube—increasing your overall earnings potential with YouTube. You’ll also earn a competitive commission when fans buy products on the retailer’s site. Spend more time creating: Since you can tag products, get insights, and review earnings in YouTube Studio, you can spend more time creating content people love and less time managing links. Note: This program is currently available for eligible creators of Argentina, Brazil, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, Mexico, Philippines, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, the United States, and Vietnam. If you would like to express interest in joining early or recommend specific brands, please fill out this form . Filling out the form does not guarantee an invitation. Creator eligibility requirements Note: Eligibility criteria for the YouTube Shopping affiliate program might be different for pilot programs. To be invited to the program, you must meet these minimum creator eligibility requirements: Your channel is in the YouTube Partner Program . Your channel meets the subscriber threshold for YPP. You are based in Argentina, Brazil, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, Mexico, Philippines, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, the United States, or Vietnam. Your channel is not a music channel, an Official Artist Channel , or associated with music partners. Music partners may include music labels, distributors, publishers, or VEVO. Your channel audience isn’t set as Made for Kids , and your channel doesn’t have a significant number of videos set as made for kids. How to join Once you have qualified to join the program, you can sign up in YouTube Studio. Sign in to YouTube Studio . From the left menu, select Earn . Under Programs , click Join Now . Review and accept the YouTube Shopping Affiliate Program Terms of Service. You’re in! You can start tagging products in your content. Learn how to tag products and be sure to follow the tagging guidelines . Add the “YouTube Shopping” extension Note: Currently, this feature is only available in the United States, India, Indonesia, South Korea, Thailand, and Vietnam on the Chrome Web Store. NEW: YouTube Shopping Chrome Extension You can easily save products to tag your video later and check out commission rates with the extension. Add the “ YouTube Shopping ” extension and get started. Learn more on how to install and manage extensions . Affiliate hub Find the growing list of participating affiliate sellers and offers where you can view their commission percentages and learn more. You can request product samples, view products that are on sale, and find higher commissions from sellers. Sign in to YouTube Studio . From the left menu, click Earn Shopping Explore affiliate offers . Select View more to see more products and sellers. If custom commission rates are available for your channel, you’ll see them under Special offer promotions . If you don’t see Special offer promotions , there are no custom commission rates available for your channel. Note: Select a seller for more details. You can also sort the sellers alphabetically or by highest commission. Use custom commission rates for affiliate campaigns Merchants can create custom commission rates to give you higher, exclusive commission rates compared to standard affiliate rates. You can find custom commission rates in the Shopping tab or through notifications in your YouTube Studio inbox. Manage and track your campaign participation Review eligible products: Open the Campaign Detail page in your Shopping Hub to see the specific products and rates. Activate higher rates: Tag eligible products in your videos. The elevated rates display in the Studio editor when you tag the products during an active campaign window. Decline or ignore offers: If a campaign does not fit your channel, simply do not tag the products. The campaign may apply automatically to eligible transactions if you tag the products, but you are never required to participate. You can track how your affiliate campaigns with custom commission rates perform by checking your affiliate metrics . Affiliate sellers lists You can start tagging products directly in YouTube Studio from the growing list of sellers that are available. Learn how to tag products . Understand your earnings Note: Commissions may fluctuate due to customer actions like order cancellations, full refunds, or exchanges during the settlement period, as the payment is only finalized after the transaction is complete and verified. To help you get started with your earnings as we launch in your market, we are currently offering a temporary incentive program with higher commission rates, by ensuring that 100% of commissions derived from affiliate merchants will go to creators. Please note that this higher rate is temporary as we grow the program, and rates may reduce in the future as we transition to a sustainable program structure. We will notify you at least 30 days before this incentive program ends. Every participating brand and retailer will set their commission rates and attribution window for each product. Commission percentages will be displayed by each product offer. Once a viewer clicks on your tagged product and makes a purchase on the retailer’s site, you may earn a commission. For more information on how commission rates are set and why they change, learn more about how to understand commission rates in the YouTube Shopping Affiliate Program . Note: Commissions earned will be paid through AdSense for YouTube within 60 to 120 days after purchase to account for customer returns. If a customer returns the product, commissions will be reversed. Bonus period You may earn a bonus based on the total sales of your tagged products between January 19 - February 19, 2026, minus the value of any returns made within the 45 days after the bonus period ends. The bonus tiers are not additive. You will earn the bonus amount of the highest tier of sales driven. Bonus sales tiers Note: This performance bonus tier is specific to eligible creators in Vietnam. Eligible creators must meet the following sales tiers to earn one bonus per month (amounts in USD): $10 Bonus for driving $30 in total product sales. $25 Bonus for driving $200 in total product sales. $85 Bonus for driving $700 in total product sales. $240 Bonus for driving $2,000+ in total product sales. Bonus payment Notwithstanding the YouTube Affiliate Program Terms of Service, (i) the earned bonus payment will be calculated based on your total sales, minus the value of any returns made within the 45 days after the bonus period ends, and (ii) your earned bonus payment will show up in your AdSense account within 120 days of the program ending each month. Note: The YouTube Shopping affiliate performance bonus program is governed by the YouTube Affiliate Program Terms of Service. Google may terminate the YouTube Shopping affiliate performance bonus program at any time with notice. Google reserves the right to deny payment for reasons including transactions generated through deceptive, abusive, fraudulent or other invalid means as determined by Google or for any other reason specified in the YouTube Affiliate Program Terms of Service. Troubleshooting sign-up issues Common approaches to resolve errors during signing up Here are common approaches to resolve errors you may encounter when signing up: Your channel may not be eligible. Review the eligibility criteria above to confirm whether your channel meets the requirements. If your channel is managed by a content owner, you must have the content owner accept the agreement on your behalf. You may be signed in to the wrong YouTube Channel. Switch to the channel you believe to be eligible and try again. Note: After confirming your issue does not fall into one of the scenarios above, contact Creator Support for further assistance. Best practices and resources Get the most from the affiliate program To get the most out of the affiliate program from day one, we recommend: Feature sales & promos from popular brands: Check if the product that you want to feature is available for commission and plan your content around sales & promos most relevant to you. Tag relevant products and use a CTA to drive sales: Tag your content with products featured within your video or Short and use a call to action to let your audience know they can shop. Use the Bulk Tagging feature to tag products across videos: In the Earn section of YouTube Studio, go to the Shopping tab Tag Products Review the videos with suggested products to tag that were found in the video's description. To tag certain products, click the product image click TAG next to the relevant products or TAG ALL for all products. To tag products for one or more videos, select the relevant videos or select all videos TAG . Get inspiration from brands and creators: Check out affiliate content from YouTube creators for content inspiration.
