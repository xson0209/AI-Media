Overview of the expanded YouTube Partner Program - Computer - YouTube Help Skip to main content Overview of the expanded YouTube Partner Program We’ve expanded the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) to more creators with earlier access to fan funding and Shopping features. The expanded YouTube Partner Program is available to eligible creators in these countries/regions . If you’re in one of these countries/regions, you can read the article below to learn more about the changes to YPP. If you’re not in one of the available countries/regions , there are no changes to the YouTube Partner Program for you. You can view this article for the YPP overview, eligibility, and application instructions relevant to you. Check your eligibility for the expanded YouTube Partner Program. If you’re not eligible yet, select Get notified in the Earn area of YouTube Studio. We’ll send you an email once the expanded YPP program is available to you and you’ve reached the eligibility thresholds. We’ve expanded the YouTube Partner Program to more creators with earlier access to fan funding and Shopping features. Creators in eligible countries can apply to the expanded YouTube Partner Program when they reach either of the below eligibility thresholds: Get 500 subscribers with 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and 3,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months, or Get 500 subscribers with 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and 3 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days If you’d like us to tell you when you’re eligible, select Get notified in the Earn area of YouTube Studio. We’ll send you an email once we’ve rolled the expanded YPP program to you and you’ve reached the above eligibility thresholds. Creators in the YouTube Partner Program who also meet the below eligibility thresholds can get additional benefits like revenue sharing from ads and YouTube Premium: Get 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months, or Get 1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified public Shorts views in the last 90 days. For partners that are currently in YPP, there are no changes to program benefits for you. What you need to join the expanded YouTube Partner Program Follow the YouTube channel monetization policies . Have a channel based in one of the available countries/regions . Make sure 2-Step Verification is turned on for your Google Account. Have one active AdSense for YouTube account , that you’ll link to your channel. If you don’t already have one, be ready to set one up in YouTube Studio when applying. Make sure to only create a new AdSense for YouTube account in YouTube Studio . In the left menu, click Earn . Click Apply Now to review and Accept the base terms. Click Start to set up an AdSense for YouTube account, or link an existing active one. Once you’re done, In Progress will surface in the Get Reviewed step, which means we have your application! What we review Our automated systems and human reviewers will review your channel as a whole to make sure your channel follows all of our policies and guidelines. Check back in the Earn section of YouTube Studio anytime to see the status of your application. All YPP applications are serviced in the order that we get them. We’ll get back to you with a decision once your channel is reviewed ( typically in about 1 month ). Keep in mind delays are possible due to: Application volumes System issues Resource limitations Channels that require a few reviews, especially when several reviewers disagree on a channel’s suitability for YPP If your first application wasn’t successful, don’t worry - keep uploading original content and you can try again after a 30-day period. If this isn’t your first application to be rejected, you can try again after a 90-day period. Our reviewers likely found that a significant portion of your channel doesn’t currently follow our policies and guidelines. So be sure to review our policies and guidelines against your channel’s overall content and change your channel before re-applying. Learn more about steps that you can take to strengthen your channel for next time. Choose how to earn and get paid Once you’re in YPP, you can start your earning journey with fan funding and Shopping features. To turn on fan funding and Shopping features, review and accept the Commerce Products Module. Learn more about the module and how to turn on fan funding and Shopping features. Fan funding & Shopping features When you join YPP with 500 subscribers, you can earn money through these monetization features if you meet their eligibility requirements: Channel memberships : lets viewers join your channel through monthly payments and get access to members-only perks you offer, like badges, emoji, and other goods. Super Chat & Super Stickers : your fans can buy Super Chats to highlight their message within live chat or Super Stickers to get a fun animated image that surfaces in live chat. Super Thanks : lets you earn revenue from viewers who want to show extra gratitude for your videos. Jewels and gifts : your fans can purchase Jewels to send a range of gifts during eligible creators’ vertical and horizontal live streams. This makes it easier for fans to react, express excitement, and actively participate in the moment. Shopping : lets you connect your official merchandise store to YouTube, showcase your products, and promote products from other brands in your content.
